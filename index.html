Ch. 03 — The Artist

Kway Cush

A self trained artist and producer who found his voice through much trial and error. He has made it a habit and tradition to explore all facets of musicianship. Kway creates music that feels both contemporary and personal. His work is recognized for its emotional depth, lyrical prose, and rich cultural textures, creating soundscapes that carry a lasting meaning.
